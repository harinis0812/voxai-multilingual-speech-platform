# VoxAI – Multilingual Speech Recognition and Synthesis Platform

**Project Overview**

VoxAI is an AI-powered multilingual speech platform developed as part of The Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI. It combines Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) capabilities into a single, user-friendly web application.

**Problem Statement**

Language barriers and limited accessibility to speech technologies make digital communication difficult for many users. There is a need for an accessible platform that can recognize speech and generate natural-sounding audio across Indian languages.

**Proposed Solution**

VoxAI integrates Gnani AI's Vachana Cloud APIs to provide multilingual speech recognition and synthesis through an interactive web interface.

**Key Features**
- Speech-to-Text: Converts spoken audio into text.
- Text-to-Speech: Generates speech from user-provided text.
- Multilingual support: Supports languages including Tamil, English and Hindi.
- Interactive interface: Provides an intuitive experience for speech operations.
- Audio processing and validation for reliable input and output.

**Technology Stack**
- Frontend: React.js, Vite
- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- AI Integration: Gnani Vachana Cloud STT and TTS APIs
- Deployment: Render

**Expected Impact**

VoxAI aims to make speech technology more accessible, simplify voice-based interactions and help reduce language barriers through AI-powered speech recognition and synthesis.
